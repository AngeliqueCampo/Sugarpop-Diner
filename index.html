Our Story

SugarPop Café began with a single dream and a whole lot of buttercream. What started as a tiny home kitchen operation grew into a cozy neighborhood spot where people come not just for cupcakes—but for a little joy in every bite.

The name "SugarPop" was inspired by our founder’s childhood nickname, given by her grandmother who always said she was “as sweet and bubbly as a sugar pop.” That spark of sweetness and fun became the heart of our café.

Every cupcake is a tribute to that warmth—handcrafted with love, laughter, and just a hint of magic. With every swirl of frosting, we aim to capture a little moment of joy — whether it's for a birthday, a breakup, or just because.

Today, we continue to mix nostalgia with creativity, crafting flavors that surprise and comfort, all while staying true to our roots: homemade happiness, shared one cupcake at a time.

A Timeline of Sweet Success

  • 2020: SugarPop was born from a late-night cupcake idea.
  • 2021: Opened our first stall at the weekend market.
  • 2022: Launched our full dine-in bakery café.
  • 2023: Became a local favorite with over 9 signature flavors.
  • 2024: Introduced nationwide delivery — spreading joy, one cupcake at a time!
